How We Work
Our Process
From Concept To Completion
Five Steps.
Zero Surprises.

Every Link Renovations project follows the same proven process — designed to keep you informed, in control, and genuinely excited every step of the way. Here's how it works.

01

Free Consultation

We start with a no-obligation conversation about your space, your vision, and your goals. We listen, take measurements, and discuss what's possible — and what isn't.

02

Design & Vision

We work closely with you on design choices, materials, and layout. You'll get product recommendations and design options that maximize value for your dollar.

03

Written Quote

You receive a detailed written quote covering materials, installation, and labor — itemized, transparent, and aligned to your budget. No hidden fees, no surprise charges.

04

Craftsmanship & Build

We get to work — treating your home as if it were our own. Daily updates, clear communication, and obsessive attention to every detail throughout the build.

05

The Reveal

We complete every job in a timely manner, do a thorough walkthrough with you, and don't consider a project finished until it exceeds your expectations.

"We treat your home as if we are working in our own."
— That's the Link Renovations promise.
