Who We Are
About Link Renovations
Our Story
Built On Craftsmanship
& Trust.

Link Renovations specializes in high-quality remodeling for residential and commercial properties in Louisville, KY and the surrounding areas. We are fully insured and treat your home as if we are working in our own.

We strive to complete every job in a timely manner, at a reasonable cost, with attention to design detail and clear client communication. As a result, a large portion of our business comes from repeat clientele and referrals.

From custom tile and marble bathrooms to full kitchen renovations, custom woodworking, decks, and commercial projects — every job receives the same level of care and craftsmanship.

Materials & Craftsmanship
The Standard.

Every Link Renovations project is held to the same uncompromising standard — the kind that turns first-time clients into repeat clients, and repeat clients into referrals.

Premium Materials
Large-format slab marble, custom hardwood millwork, hand-selected stone, and architectural-grade hardware. We specify and source for longevity, not for the spec sheet.
